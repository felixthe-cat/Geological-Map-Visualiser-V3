# UX Changelog

What changed for you, newest at the bottom of each day. Append only.

- 2026-08-30 — You can now carry the soil and rock layers past the outermost borehole to the
  ends of the section line. Choose to leave that stretch blank (as before), extend the layers
  flat, or extend them on the trend of the last two boreholes. A grey dotted line marks where
  the real logged data stops.
- 2026-08-30 — New ground surface option: the terrain is corrected using the difference
  between each borehole's surveyed ground level and the terrain model at that borehole's own
  location. Because an off-line borehole was drilled somewhere else, the ground line no longer
  passes exactly through its collar — the note on screen tells you how far apart they are.
- 2026-08-30 — Downloaded images are now named after the diagram title you typed, then the
  kind of drawing, then the date — for example `My-Site_Cross-Section_2026-08-30.png`. This
  applies to both the site plan and the cross-section.
- 2026-08-30 — You can now draw a labelled rectangle on the cross-section to show where a
  proposed structure sits. Drag it to move it, rotate it by degrees, or type its four corners
  as exact chainage and level values.
- 2026-08-30 — Click any borehole on the site plan to leave it out of the cross-section, and
  click it again to put it back. Excluded boreholes turn grey, the same as boreholes outside
  the search corridor. A "Re-include all" button resets them.
- 2026-08-30 — Move the cursor across the cross-section and a dotted vertical line follows it,
  with a panel listing the ground level and the top level and thickness of every soil and rock
  layer at that exact point.
- 2026-08-30 — Saving a project now stores your whole cross-section setup, not just the
  borehole data: the section line, the site boundary, every option setting, the boreholes you
  deselected and any annotations you drew. Reopening the project restores all of it.
- 2026-09-10 — Fixed: choosing OpenStreetMap on the Site Map showed a blank map once you had
  zoomed in close to your site. It now works at every zoom level.
- 2026-09-10 — The Site Map now also offers the Esri satellite base map, matching the choices
  on the Cross-Section and Rock Contour tabs.
- 2026-09-10 — Boreholes with AGS stratigraphy are easier to see: the green markers are now
  solid with a dark outline instead of partly see-through.
- 2026-09-10 — The cross-section now opens with the offset-corrected ground surface already
  selected. Projects you saved earlier still reopen with whatever setting you saved them with.
