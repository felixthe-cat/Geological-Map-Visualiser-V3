Key technical facts for whoever continues:
- LandsD 3D tiles (`data.map.gov.hk/api/3d-data/3dtiles/f2/`) need **no key**, and send CORS
  headers for the Vercel origin and for localhost. Two quirks are handled in `builder3d.js`:
  - `+` in tile names → `%2B` (a `preprocessURL` plugin);
  - the textures are KTX2 images without `KHR_texture_basisu` → the `KTX2ImagePlugin` glTF plugin.
- The photo-mesh is auto-levelled against DTM + collar levels using the 85th percentile
  (canopy biases hits upward). The hillside example gives −1.07 m.
- `flattenTile()`:
  - inside the boundary, the mesh is laid on the block's ground surface (+5 cm);
  - outside, anything more than 2 m above ground is lowered: fully within 10 m, fading out
    over 20 m (`TREE_H`, `CLEAR`, `TAPER`);
  - originals are kept for the toggle and for re-levelling.
